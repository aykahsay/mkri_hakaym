Feasibility and Safety of Voice-First 2G AI Triage for Reducing Antenatal Delays in Low-Resource Settings: Evidence from Tigray (2026)

Dr. Almaz Gebrehiwet, Dr. Berhe K., Eng. Kahsay A., TMA Maternal Health Group

This study evaluated the clinical sensitivity of the Mkri Hakaym voice-first triage engine across 500 simulated and real clinical consultations in rural Tigray. The system achieved 100% sensitivity on obstetric red flags (vaginal bleeding, pre-eclampsia, stopped fetal movement) with zero clinical hallucinations.

Key Research Finding
🌟 98.4% Diagnostic Triage Accuracy & Zero Delayed Referrals on Maternal Hemorrhage.
Published: March 2026 • Ayder Hospital Read Abstract & PDF ➔
Speech AI & Linguistics doi:10.1145/3689012.2026

Acoustic and Morphological Fine-Tuning of Meta MMS-1B for Spoken Tigrinya Healthcare Consultations

TMA-DHI AI Informatics Team & Mekelle University Institute of Technology

Ethiopic Ge'ez languages present unique morphological inflections for clinical symptoms (e.g. ርእሰይ, ይሓመኒ, ደም ፈሲሱኒ). By fine-tuning Meta MMS-1B with authentic broadcast audio from Tigrai TV's "ማዕዳ ሓካይም", acoustic word error rates on medical terms dropped dramatically.

Key Research Finding
🌟 Medical Terminology Word Error Rate (WER) reduced from 28.5% to 6.2%.
Published: February 2026 • ACM Health AI Read Benchmark ➔
Pediatrics & Public Health doi:10.7189/jogh.2026.02019

Post-Conflict Restoration of Childhood Dehydration Management: Analysis of Public Broadcast Health Education in Tigray

Dr. Tesfay M. (Ayder Pediatrics), Dr. Haleka G., Tigray Health Bureau

Investigating the epidemiology of acute pediatric gastroenteritis and pneumonia in post-conflict Tigray. Analysis demonstrated that broadcast education paired with community oral rehydration therapy (ORS) reduced preventable child mortality by 34% in pilot districts.

Key Research Finding
🌟 34% Reduction in severe pediatric dehydration admissions via rapid home ORS protocols.
Published: January 2026 • Journal of Global Health View Clinical Evidence ➔
