Focus: Maternal, Pediatric & Red-Flag Triage
Clinical Pilot P-2026-002 • Telemedicine

Ayder Tele-Consult Network

Connecting rural district health centers in Shire, Axum, Adigrat, and Abi Adi with sub-specialty clinics at Ayder Comprehensive Specialized Hospital.

Lead: Dr. Teklehaimanot G. (Mekelle University)
Focus: Remote Radiology & Pediatric Referrals
In Development P-2026-003 • Health Informatics

Tigrinya Clinical EMR & Ontology

Standardized Tigrinya clinical terminology mapping to ICD-11 and SNOMED-CT for offline-first rural health post electronic medical records.

Lead: Eng. Kahsay Berhe (Health Informatics)
Focus: Clinical Vocabularies & FHIR Interoperability
Proposed P-2026-004 • Maternal Health

Maternal & Infant Surveillance (MIRS)

Community-level surveillance detecting high-risk pre-eclampsia, postpartum hemorrhage, and neonatal sepsis before complications become fatal.

✍️ Membership Governance

Multi-stage verification: credential review by TMA leadership, separating professional identities (persons) from authentication (user_accounts).

  • ✔ Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
  • ✔ Project-scoped authority (no global privilege escalation)
  • ✔ Verified medical & engineering credentials

🔒 Strict Dataset Licensing

Raw clinical speech corpora and transcribed records are gated behind paid commercial and academic licensing. Proceeds directly sustain healthcare delivery.

  • ✔ Free downloading strictly forbidden (HTTP 402 enforced)
  • ✔ Integrated Telebirr, CBE Birr & Stripe payments
  • ✔ Cryptographic license keys with download quota tracking

🛡️ Security & Relational Schema

Built on PostgreSQL with 24 relational entities, strict foreign key constraints, Row-Level Security (RLS), and append-only audit logs.

  • ✔ Password hashing with Argon2id
  • ✔ End-to-end audit logs on administrative decisions
  • ✔ Strict medical data de-identification protocols
